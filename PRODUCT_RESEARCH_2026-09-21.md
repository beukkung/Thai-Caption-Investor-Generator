# Product Research — 21 Sep 2026

## Question

How should the original Thai investor caption generator be changed to reach a mass Thai audience?

## External signals

### 1. Caption demand is broad and proven

Large Thai publishers continue to publish and refresh caption collections in 2026 across general funny captions, love, cafe/food, travel, work, and other moments.

Examples observed during research:
- TrueID general funny captions: page displays 18.7M views
- TrueID cafe/coffee captions: page displays 4.1M views
- TrueID love captions: page displays 1.3M views
- TrueID travel-English captions: page displays 1.5M views
- TrueID temple/travel captions: page displays 3.8M views

These numbers are page-displayed metrics, not independently audited traffic figures, but they are useful directional evidence that caption search intent is much broader than investing.

Sources:
- https://women.trueid.net/detail/x8e4977Ozkr6
- https://food.trueid.net/detail/q28B6G1Obn5P
- https://women.trueid.net/detail/R5xpxlgoyOX5/
- https://travel.trueid.net/detail/y86bPD5RDBz8
- https://travel.trueid.net/detail/7mDwjXqgYK2R

### 2. Thailand is an unusually large social-content market

DataReportal's Digital 2026 Thailand report says Thailand had 56.6M social media user identities in Oct 2025. It also reports large local audiences for LINE, Facebook, TikTok, Instagram, and other platforms.

Source:
- https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-thailand

### 3. Generic global caption generators are already commoditized

Current global tools commonly offer platform selection, tone/style, description/keywords, or image-to-caption workflows.

Examples:
- Hootsuite AI Caption Generator
- Canva Image to Caption

Sources:
- https://www.hootsuite.com/social-media-tools/ai-caption-generator-social-media
- https://www.canva.com/features/image-to-caption/

Implication: competing as “another AI caption generator” is weak positioning.

## Product decision

### Positioning

**Thai-first + moment-first**, not AI-first.

Working consumer brand:
> แคปชั่นไหนดี?

Core promise:
> รูปพร้อมแล้ว เหลือแค่แคปชั่น

### Information architecture

Mass categories:
1. กวน / ฮา
2. รัก / โสด
3. กิน / คาเฟ่
4. เที่ยว / วันหยุด
5. งาน / มนุษย์เงินเดือน
6. เงิน / ออม
7. ลงทุน
8. ชีวิต / กำลังใจ

Finance/investing is retained as a differentiated strength, not the entire market definition.

## Distribution decision

Use an **SEO + interactive tool** model.

Search pages solve acquisition. The generator solves personalization. Copy/share actions solve utility and may later create distribution loops.

Do not create hundreds of thin SEO pages. Start with a small number of useful pages, measure impressions/clicks, then expand only where demand appears.

## V2 hypotheses

H1: Users prefer 3 choices over a single random output.

H2: Moment-first categories reduce first-use friction compared with a blank prompt.

H3: General-life categories produce more sessions than investing-only categories.

H4: Copy rate is a better product-value signal than generation count.

H5: SEO landing pages can become the main acquisition channel before paid marketing.

## Validation sequence

### Phase 1 — Static launch
- Deploy the V2 prototype
- Add privacy-friendly analytics
- Track category, tone, generate, copy, share

### Phase 2 — Search validation
- Submit to Search Console
- Track impressions by page/query
- Improve only pages receiving real impressions

### Phase 3 — Product validation
- Identify top 2–3 moments by copy rate
- Expand caption depth for those moments
- Test seasonal packs: payday, Friday, birthday, New Year, Songkran

### Phase 4 — AI only where needed
Possible later upgrade:
- user describes photo/context
- image-to-caption
- personal voice presets
- creator/business mode

AI should improve a proven workflow, not be the reason the product exists.

## North-star metric candidate

**Useful Caption Sessions = sessions with at least one copy or share**

This measures whether the product actually helped a user finish the job.
